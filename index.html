Who we are

A health initiative led by the communities it serves

Victoria's Wellness Initiative for Women and Girls is a community-driven nonprofit. We began because too many women and girls could not reach good health information or care. We work to change that through education, prevention and local action, so every woman and girl can live a healthy life with dignity.

See our programmes

Our mission

To transform the health and wellbeing of women and girls.

We do this by building health knowledge, widening access to quality care and prevention, and giving underserved communities the resources and opportunities to thrive.

Our vision

A world where every woman and girl, wherever she lives, is informed, healthy and able to live with dignity, purpose and possibility.

Our values

What guides our work

Ten promises we keep in every outreach, every session and every conversation.

  • Empowerment

    We give women and girls the knowledge, confidence and resources to make informed choices about health and life.

  • Health Equity

    Good healthcare and health information are rights, not privileges.

  • Prevention

    Early education, screening, vaccination and healthy living help problems get caught in time.

  • Dignity and Compassion

    Every woman and girl deserves to be seen, heard, respected and cared for.

  • Knowledge and Health Literacy

    We turn reliable health information into practical knowledge people can use.

  • Inclusion

    We make safe, respectful spaces where every woman and girl can take part.

  • Community-Centred Impact

    We listen to communities and build solutions that answer real needs.

  • Integrity and Accountability

    We practise openness, ethics and careful use of every contribution.

  • Collaboration

    We work with communities, health professionals, governments and partners.

  • Sustainable Transformation

    We help communities grow stronger so change lasts beyond short-term help.
